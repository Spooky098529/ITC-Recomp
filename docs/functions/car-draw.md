# Known functions: the car draw

The car scene's per-pass draw of one scene model (pool 7 of the second
set, `second-set.md`; the scene in `scene.md`): `Car::DrawUpdate`
(`sub_820DAC68`), which the scene's draw (`sub_820C5DE8`, `car-scene-draw.md`
since 2026-11-04) calls for each scene model on each of its passes, with the
scene's current pass description at scene + 5072 (the camera pass, the
render queue's sort keys, a mask of what the pass draws). Before drawing
it lowers the boost (block +704) by 1.0, culls the car against the
pass's camera and by height against the player's car, runs the load
poll's finishers and apply steps itself, each behind its part's ready
flag (+5052 to +5104; `SceneModel::PollLoad` does them at once,
`load-poll.md`), so a car that arrives mid-race is finished from the
draw, and swaps in the second wheels once they arrive (releasing the
old ones, `ScenePools::ReleaseB`). Then it draws the model's second-set
objects, each by its own point block (`second-set.md`) and the scene
model's 736-byte block of matrices, flags, angles and alphas
(`SceneModelBlock`, the model's +4288): the light kit (pool 4: the
neons, LEDs and spots by its record's modes, with blink timers), the
parts (pool 1: the bumpers, doors, mirrors, bonnet, fenders, cabin,
engine, number plates, the head and tail lights with their
camera-facing glows, and the shadow shapes into the depth passes,
plain and flattened away from up to three nearby lights), the wheels
(pool 5: tires, rims and brakes on both sides, morphed once at set-up
or, by the wheels record, at every draw through a worker thread job),
the interior (pool 3: body, dial faces, the needles and digital
speedometer, seats, steering wheel, roll cage, the accessory), the
traffic body (pool 6) and, for the cockpit view's model, only the
cockpit (pool 2). The front end (the scene's model set 0) and the race
(set 1) have their own lists for the parts and the interior. Every part
goes through `CarPart::Draw` and `Model::Draw`, which records the
object (`Recorder::RecordObject`, `scenery-lists.md`) and hands each
material group to the render queue (`RenderQueue::Add`,
`device-shaders.md`) under the pass's sort keys.

Read by the B7 loop's mapping agent (2026-11-01) from the lead of
`Car::DrawUpdate`, with the lead of the second set's objects' per-frame
passes folded in; the reading was split among six subagents, run one at
a time. Read in full except where a row says otherwise. Shared, with rows
elsewhere: the finishers and apply steps (`load-poll.md`), the release
(`ScenePools::ReleaseB`, `functions.md`), the material passes
(`PartModel::SetAlpha`, `SetPair1002Alpha`, `SetMaterials`,
`SetTexture`, `FlipCulling`, `Matrix::TransposeRotation`,
`Projector::Matrices`, `TableVector` `sub_820D35D0`;
`material-passes.md`), the model's recorders and depth draws
(`Model::IsShapeVisible`, `Recorder::RecordObject`,
`Recorder::RecordSceneLights`, `Model::RecordShapeDepth`,
`Model::DrawShapeDepth`; `scenery-lists.md`), `Model::MaterialCount` and
`Model::RecordMaterials` (`models.md`), `RenderQueue::Add`
(`device-shaders.md`), the morph (`Morph::SetUp`, `Morph::Blend`;
`second-set.md`), `Lights::FindNearest` (`course-scenery.md`), the
matrix math (`matrix-math.md`), `memcpy` and `cos` (`c-runtime.md`),
`XPR::GetTexture` (`functions.md`), `Accessory::SetTexture` and
`Accessory::Draw` (`accessories.md`), `sub_820F0E50`
(`scene-models.md`), the cockpit dials' `Dash::PoseTachometer` and
`Dash::PoseDials` (`functions.md`, native under
`--native_cockpit_dials`, which call `CarPart::Draw` from inside the
interior's race draw). Not here: the scene's draw `sub_820C5DE8` and the
pass description's set-up `sub_820C4EB8` (`car-scene-draw.md` since 2026-11-04), the morph's worker job
(`sub_820AC7F0`, the second pool maker's slot 1, and the job's methods
at `820AC998`-`820ACC08`; [`morph-jobs.md`](morph-jobs.md) since
2026-11-13). Fields: `../known-data.md` ("scene
model", "scene model block", "scene pass description", "second set
objects", "car draw records", "work memory").

What wraps or reads these functions, kept around native bodies by the
migration (the precedent of the load poll and `XPR::GetTexture`):
`Car::DrawUpdate`, `SceneModel::IsNearPlayerHeight`,
`SceneCockpit::Draw`, `SceneLights::Draw`, `SceneParts::Draw`,
`SceneInterior::Draw`, `SceneTraffic::Draw` and `SceneWheels::Draw` are
Wrapped by the diagnostics (`ITC_FRAME_STATE_WRAP` in
`src/debug/frame_state_diff.cpp`, `--find_per_frame_state`,
`--debug_hide`); `BlinkTimers::Advance` is skipped on render-only passes
(`--per_tick_blink_timers`, `src/frame_pacing/per_frame_logic.cpp`);
`CarPart::Draw` is called by the native cockpit dials
(`src/hooks/cockpit_dials.cpp`, around `BlendState_SetLivePose`);
`Model::Draw`'s call of the per-object recorder returns to `0x820D0564`
with r31 the model, which frame interpolation reads to pair products by
object (`--pair_by_object`, `src/interpolation/blend_state.cpp`), so a
native `Model::Draw` keeps both, as the native scenery draws keep the
recorder's return addresses (`src/scenery_lists/model_draws.cpp`); and
`src/hooks/lights.cpp` relies on `CarLighting::Gather` reading only r3
after `Lights::FindNearest` (`0x820C92F8`). The light glows call
`Model::Draw` directly with one shared glow model (the scene's +480) for
every car, so `--pair_by_object` sees them under one object.

Things the draw writes that outlive the pass (to keep as the original
does): the boost, the finishers' flags and the second wheels' swap; the
door and headlight matrices (the parts' door groups' +6016, the
headlights group's +3120) that later draws in the same pass use; the
number plates' texture word (+456) and the gauges' and digits' texture
providers; the shared glow model's materials and alpha; the light kit's
blink counters; the morphed draws' vertex memory, job and the shapes'
+412 to +428; the brake group's copied rows (+2912, +2928), the tire
group's weights; the render queue keys at `0x8296B8F8` to `0x8296B90C`
(saved and put back). Original quirks to copy: the rear bumper's draw
writes guest words 432 and 456 when it has no plate model (a null
model); the race's shadow draws read lighting words left unwritten
outside a race; the front tire's second matrix stays the identity and is
still drawn; the tire's depth pass draws its second rear tire with the
main camera.

Constants: `[0x82000A30]` is 1.0, `[0x82000A34]` 0.0, `[0x82002240]` π,
`[0x82002948]` π/180, `[0x82002C34]` 180.0, `[0x82002D0C]` 25.4,
`[0x82006634]` 1/9 (read from the image). `[0x826C2E44]` is the scene.

Made native by the B7 loop's migration (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-04, in 16
parts, one agent at a time; brief `../loops/car-draw-brief.md`), from the
leaves up to the root, under one flag, `--native_car_draw` (on by
default; each adapter falls back to the original when the flag or its
callees' flags are off): plain C++ in `src/car_draw/` (`car_draw.h`; the
root in `update.cpp`) behind the register steps in `src/hooks/car_draw.cpp`,
checked by the `car_draw` harness (`src/harnesses/car_draw*.cpp`, the
root's cases in `car_draw_root.cpp`, run last with a random generator of
their own). Every function in play that's ours is Native but these, which
keep an override: `Car::DrawUpdate`, `SceneModel::IsNearPlayerHeight` and
the six objects' tops (`SceneLights::Draw`, `SceneParts::Draw`,
`SceneWheels::Draw`, `SceneInterior::Draw`, `SceneCockpit::Draw`,
`SceneTraffic::Draw`) are the diagnostics' wraps with the native body
inside (`Car::DrawUpdate`'s caller, the scene's draw `sub_820C5DE8`, is
recompiled; the others' only callers are native now, and the native root

its calls of them); `CarPart::Draw`'s adapter is what the native cockpit
dials call; `BlinkTimers::Advance`'s is its render-only skip. Left as
guest calls from the native frames, never seen in play: the light kit's
LEDs and spots with `LightKit::ChaseLevel`, `Accessory::Draw`, and
`Model::SkinShape` (an accessory's skin, not reached from here); the
morph's worker job (`sub_820AC7F0`, `morph-jobs.md`) and the cockpit dials'
`Dash::PoseTachometer` and `Dash::PoseDials` (native under
`--native_cockpit_dials`) are also called from the original's frames.

Status:

- **Native**: plain C++ in `itc_recomp/src/` that doesn't use the
  PowerPC context (`ctx`): normal arguments and host structs (it may
  still read and write guest memory through `src/guest.h` while
  recompiled code shares the data). The game reaches it without `ctx`,
  from native or overridden callers, and the recompiled original is no
  longer called (except behind a fallback flag or to check against it).
  No `REX_FUNC` override of it exists, not even an adapter kept only for
  a harness (that's Overridden until the adapter goes).
  The end state for every function.
- **Overridden**: an `extern "C" REX_FUNC(sub_XXXXXXXX)` override in
  `itc_recomp/src/` (or a macro defining one) does the work instead of
  the original: a thin adapter in front of native code, or a body that
  still uses `ctx` (calling guest code, reading registers). It may call
  the original when its flag is off, or to check itself against it.
- **Wrapped**: an override that does something of its own (skipping
  render-only passes, logging, timing) and still calls the original
  (`__imp__sub_XXXXXXXX`) for the work.
- **Dropped**: its work isn't needed on PC (hardware set-up, kernel calls
  the SDK already stubbed), so it no longer runs: an override in
  `itc_recomp/src/` replaces it with nothing (or a fixed return value), or
  its callers, made native, leave the call out (no override then; the row
  says which caller). Logs, a harness or the renderer's frame dumps have
  shown that nothing reads what it did. The original is no longer called,
  except behind a fallback flag while the drop is being checked. A
  function the game never calls in the first place isn't Dropped: its
  status stays blank and its row says it's never called.
- **Merged**: its work is done inside a native caller's plain C++ (a
  few lines only that caller needs), so it has no function or override of
  its own in `itc_recomp/src/`. The row names the caller(s) and the
  harness that covers the work. The original is no longer called, except
  behind the caller's fallback flag.
- **Patched**: the generated code was edited in place (search for
  `PATCH:`).

A function usually moves from recompiled or Wrapped to Overridden to
Native as the `ctx` edge moves up through its callers, or straight to
Dropped when its work isn't needed.
`itc_recomp/tools/check_function_statuses.py` checks this column, in
every file of the tables, against `src/` (an override marked Native, a
status with no override, an override with no row, a row in two files);
run it after changing either, on any branch.
