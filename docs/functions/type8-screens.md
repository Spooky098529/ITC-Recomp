# Known functions: the type 8 mode's panel and list screens

Three of the screens of the front-end mode of **type 8** (`Mode::Create`
in [`game-modes.md`](game-modes.md); `ModeType8::ModeType8`, its
member's constructor and destructor in [`boot-mode.md`](boot-mode.md)),
probably the two-player (split-screen) mode: the title screen's main
menu item 4 (string 251) leaves the title with state 18, which
`ModeType4::NextMode` turns into type 8 ([`title-screen.md`](title-screen.md)).
Everything in the mode is laid out for two players: its Init (slot 0,
`sub_8223D5D8`, a lead) makes the stand scene for three cars and puts
the two players' garage cars (the two 1,972-byte entries at
`0x82814EB8`, [`car-makers.md`](car-makers.md)) on garage stages 1 and
2; the second player's save data is `[0x826C5B5C]` (the two-player save
block, 0x3AB74 bytes, two halves of 120,108; `sub_82105740` and
`SaveData::TwoPlayerBlock` in [`save-data.md`](save-data.md)); its save
screen's kind 6 loads that block ([`save-screen.md`](save-screen.md));
its options are the only ones that open the controller pages
([`options-screen.md`](options-screen.md)); and both race set-ups here
add two racers from the two garage cars. The mode is never made in play
(no coverage session picked item 4), so none of these functions ran;
they get rows by the user's 2026-11-12 call.

The screens, in the mode's member (`ModeType8Member`, at the mode's
+848; the Init runs each screen's Init on it):

- The **panel screen** (`Type8PanelScreen`, member +42740, 15,252
  bytes, vtable `0x82010AF0`; UI movie 4, asset 22586): the two
  players' car pick. It is built as the type 6 and 7 panel screens
  ([`type67-screens.md`](type67-screens.md)) but with its panels
  inline, five a player (F, E, C, D and the confirm panel, each built
  with the player 0 or 1 and its own string and node ids), one panel
  group (+11212) held by `SingleGroup` (+15220), and the two-list car
  list `PanelSetPair` (+11272: its bases at +11276 and +13248, one a
  player; [`car-list.md`](car-list.md)). Its Init is the shared
  `PanelScreen::Init`; its machine's transitions
  (`Type8PanelScreen::InitMachine`) are the shared panel handlers plus
  its own: the screen opens itself (+9652 = 1, so `PanelScreen::WaitForMovie`
  goes to state 3), plays the group's opening, and (unless the mode
  before was the race) posts message 29, the save screen's kind 6 (the
  two-player save block's load, probably), whose answer (31) shows the
  pick; a player backing out asks to quit (the dialog, UI movie 16,
  messages 7, 9, 10, 12, 13) and `PickCar` copies both players' picks
  into the two garage cars.
- The **list screen** (`Type8ListScreen`, member +19544, 11,368 bytes with its part,
  vtable `0x82010E18`; UI movie `[+9680]`, asset 22584): a
  `NodeListScreen` (`type67-screens.md`) under its own base
  (`Type8ListBase`, vtable `0x82011638`: scroll texts 545 to 549 at
  +10820, its own Draw and Update over page and step tables in `.data`)
  with a race kind list (+10144, 3 items), a course list (+10216, 8
  items, 6 shown: strings 395 to 404), a two-item toggle (+10288, the
  traffic) and a list part (`Type8ListPart`, +10844) holding a panel a
  player (two menus each). Its machine is `Type6ListScreen::InitMachine`
  (shared, `type6-type7-modes.md`, the open message 14), and its race
  set-up (`SetUpRace`) puts both players' cars on the picked course.
- The **color list screen** (`Type8ColorListScreen`, member +30912,
  11,828 bytes with its part, vtable `0x82010CF0`; asset 22585): a `ColorListScreen`
  with `ColorListScreen`'s own Draw, Update, pages and steps, its own
  Init (`InitMachine`, the shared list handlers, the open message 16),
  a 9-item list (+10144), and a list part (`Type8ColorListPart`,
  +11040) with three menus a player; its `SetUpRace` picks the route
  from its lists and the two racers' words from the part's menus.

Pages and steps follow the list screens' page block (+9644 the page,
+9648 the page after the step, +9652 the page before, +9656 the step,
+9660 the queued step, +9668 the step's first-pass word), as
`type67-screens.md` describes. The list parts' inner objects keep their
own page and step tables in `.data` (`0x82637A44`/`0x82637A4C` for the
color list's, `0x82637A6C`/`0x82637A74` for the list's: two steps and
three pages each, the step 1 and pages 1 and 2 shared), over two
per-player blocks (+40 and +272, or +40 and +404): +0 the player, +4
ready (confirmed), +8 backed out, +12 and +84 (and +156) the menus. Both
players ready → page 1 (fade out, the prompt's yes); player 0 backing
out → page 2 (the prompt's back).

Status:

- **Native**: plain C++ in `itc_recomp/src/` that doesn't use the
  PowerPC context (`ctx`): normal arguments and host structs (it may
  still read and write guest memory through `src/guest.h` while
  recompiled code shares the data). The game reaches it without `ctx`,
  from native or overridden callers, and the recompiled original is no
  longer called (except behind a fallback flag or to check against it).
  No `REX_FUNC` override of it exists, not even an adapter kept only for
  a harness (that's Overridden until the adapter goes).
  The end state for every function.
- **Overridden**: an `extern "C" REX_FUNC(sub_XXXXXXXX)` override in
  `itc_recomp/src/` (or a macro defining one) does the work instead of
  the original: a thin adapter in front of native code, or a body that
  still uses `ctx` (calling guest code, reading registers). It may call
  the original when its flag is off, or to check itself against it.
- **Wrapped**: an override that does something of its own (skipping
  render-only passes, logging, timing) and still calls the original
  (`__imp__sub_XXXXXXXX`) for the work.
- **Dropped**: its work isn't needed on PC (hardware set-up, kernel calls
  the SDK already stubbed), so it no longer runs: an override in
  `itc_recomp/src/` replaces it with nothing (or a fixed return value), or
  its callers, made native, leave the call out (no override then; the row
  says which caller). Logs, a harness or the renderer's frame dumps have
  shown that nothing reads what it did. The original is no longer called,
  except behind a fallback flag while the drop is being checked. A
  function the game never calls in the first place isn't Dropped: its
  status stays blank and its row says it's never called.
- **Merged**: its work is done inside a native caller's plain C++ (a
  few lines only that caller needs), so it has no function or override of
  its own in `itc_recomp/src/`. The row names the caller(s) and the
  harness that covers the work. The original is no longer called, except
  behind the caller's fallback flag.
- **Patched**: the generated code was edited in place (search for
  `PATCH:`).

A function usually moves from recompiled or Wrapped to Overridden to
Native as the `ctx` edge moves up through its callers, or straight to
Dropped when its work isn't needed.
